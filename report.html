Report a problem

Something broke, or something’s missing. Tell me.

Bugs, confusions, things you expected Otto to do and it didn’t, things you wish it did: all of it goes in the same short form, and every answer lands in a spreadsheet I read. Three steps, the first one only if something actually went wrong.

←An idea counts. The form asks whether it’s a bug or an idea and that’s the whole difference.

1.If Otto misbehaved, save a problem report

In Otto, open Settings, find Report a problem, type a line about what you were doing, and press Save problem report (.zip). It writes one small file: your note, Otto’s log, the version, and the shape of your world (how many entries of each kind). Nothing you have written goes in it, and Otto sends it nowhere; it stays on your disk until you hand it over.

←Skip this step for an idea, or for a problem with the website.

2.Fill in the form

What happened, what you expected instead, and the version from the top of Settings. Plain words are fine; you don’t have to know what went wrong. Leave your email if you’d like a reply; leave it out if you’d rather not.

Open the form a Google form · opens in the same tab · takes a minute

3.Send the file, if you made one

The form doesn’t take files, so email the .zip to meateaapple@gmail.com with a word or two so I can match it to your form answer. No need to sign in to anything.

←The file is a few kilobytes. Open it yourself first if you like; it’s an ordinary zip.

About the form: it is hosted by Google, so Google sees what you type as you type it, the way it would on any Google form. The answers go to a spreadsheet only I can open. I use an email address only to reply, and I don’t keep a list.

Otto is made by one person. This site is set in Fraunces, EB Garamond and Courier Prime, and is a handful of plain pages with nothing hidden in them.
