The beta

The beta is the whole program, and it opens on the sample world so you have something to take apart. Start your own world beside it whenever you like. When Otto is finished it will cost money, and people who tested the beta will hear first; the build you have keeps working either way, and nothing you made goes anywhere.

←No time limit on the beta. What I’d like in return is a note about what stalled you, if anything did: report a problem.

What it will cost later

The plan, when the beta ends: you buy the program once, and it is yours to keep and run for as long as your computer will run it. For a year from the day you buy, every new version is yours as well. After the year Otto doesn’t stop, nag, or shrink. You stay on the version you have, and if a later one has something you want, you can buy another year. If not, don’t.

←There is no subscription. Nothing renews by itself.

Which computers

Windows, now. A Mac version is being made, and it will come when it is good rather than when it is due. The first time you run Otto, Windows may tell you it has “protected your PC”. Choose More info, then Run anyway.
