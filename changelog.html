Changes

Every release, newest first.

Work that has landed but not yet shipped as a numbered version sits at the top. Nothing here ever touches what you have written: each version backs your world up before it opens it.

on the way Oct 2026
  • –Writing in two windows: Ctrl-click a chapter and it opens in writer mode in a second window; the world stays in the first.
  • –Rest the pointer on a name while writing and a card shows who it is: summary, portrait, a few fields.
  • –CSV import rebuilt for the current forms: a template per form, categories, links by name (several with ;), dates, quantities, a Belongs-to-project column. Export query results back to CSV and re-import with Update.
  • –Bulk edit on query results sets links, dates and options from one box; add to or remove from a project.
  • –A window coming back to the front picks up what another window saved.
  • –Sub-units number themselves from their names; Renumber and drag-to-reorder on the parent; the sidebar follows the order.
  • –Word counts on the project page, for the unit and the whole book.
  • –Word export uses real heading styles, so Word’s navigation pane lists the chapters.
  • –The edit form autosaves 90 seconds after the last change; changing an entry’s form keeps what you had typed.
  • –The map editor remembers which form new places take; a default map per entry; show any image or upload in its folder.
  • –Query by stars; author notes can go into a Word export, marked; “Don’t count this” for pasted-in text.
0.3.1 29 Sep 2026
  • –Nine new launch illustrations.
  • –The toolbar names the entry you’re on and whether you’re viewing or editing.
  • –Notes kept with a project are listed under their book in the sidebar.
  • –Foundational Lore on every Place, Artifact and Concept form.
  • –A dry run of pending migrations before a release; World Health reports forms and categories that no longer exist.
0.3.0 17 Sep 2026
  • –Project is one form: the kind of unit (series, novel, part, chapter, scene) is its category.
  • –Story arcs and beats; Realized In links a beat to the chapter that carries it.
  • –Notes kept with a chapter, listed under it and under Research.
  • –The interface on one spacing and type scale across every page and all four themes.
  • –Reciprocal-pair badge on paired fields.
earlier 2026
  • –The taxonomy sweeps: every built-in type took its current forms, categories and vocabularies, each with a migration and an audit.
  • –Calendars that carry their dates; relative dates; the chronology on one axis.
  • –Maps with pins, regions, routes, era layers and sub-maps; the terrain generator.
  • –Writer mode, author notes, the writing log and goals; Word and Markdown export.
  • –Custom forms and categories alongside the built-in ones.
